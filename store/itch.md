## Details

Description:

Swap Meet Sprint is a one minute trading game. The morning market opens, you have $50, and the sellers are already at your table.

Items arrive one at a time: handhelds, cartridges, toy robots, lamps, records, cameras, watches and paintings. Each one shows a guide price, an asking price and the seller's face. Clues appear as you wait. The seller will tell you things too. Some of them are true.

Buy, haggle or pass. What you buy goes on your stall and sells a few seconds later for what it is really worth. Mint and boxed is a jackpot. A fake is worth almost nothing.

Play the Daily Morning, the same items for everyone each day, or Free Play for a fresh market every time. Fill the Finds shelf with 40 rare treasures.

Controls:
Mouse or touch: tap BUY, HAGGLE or PASS, or swipe right, up or left.
Keyboard: Right or D buys, Up, W or Space haggles, Left or A passes. M mutes.

Made by Bal Tak. All art, sound and the font are made in code. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere.
