## Version 1.0

Promotional text (170 at most):

One minute at the morning swap meet. Buy low, haggle hard, spot the fakes and sell for a profit. A new Daily Morning every day, the same for everyone.

Description:

Swap Meet Sprint is a one minute trading game. The market opens, you have $50, and the sellers are waiting at your table.

Items arrive one at a time: old handhelds, game cartridges, toy robots, lamps, records, cameras, watches and paintings. Each one shows a guide price, the seller's asking price and the seller's face. Clues appear while you wait. The seller tells you things too. Some sellers tell the truth.

Buy it, haggle for a better price, or pass. What you buy goes on your stall and sells a few seconds later for what it is really worth. Mint and boxed can be a jackpot. A fake is worth almost nothing.

DAILY MORNING
The same items for every player each day, so scores are fair to compare. Your first try of the day is the one that counts and the one you can share.

FREE PLAY
A fresh market every time.

COLLECT
Fill your Finds shelf with 40 rare treasures. Keep your day streak going. Chase your best score.

Thousands of invented items drawn as pixel art. Quick to learn, one tap to play, one minute a round. Plays offline.

No account. No tracking. Nothing is collected.

## App Review information

Sign in required: No.
Notes for the reviewer:

Swap Meet Sprint is a one minute single player trading game that runs fully offline. There is no account, no purchases, no adverts and no network use. The first launch opens a short guided round: the clock waits while three hint lines explain the buttons. To test quickly: tap BUY when the ask is lower than the guide, PASS otherwise. After a round, PLAY AGAIN starts another, and the house icon goes to the title screen with Daily Morning, Free Play and the Finds shelf. Haptics play on buy, sale, fake and rare finds. Share opens the system share sheet with a picture of the score.
