How to play

You have one minute and $50. Items come one at a time. Tap BUY, HAGGLE or PASS, or swipe right, up or left. What you buy sells a few seconds later for what it is really worth. Read the clues: a fake is worth almost nothing, a mint item in its box is worth a lot.

Daily Morning

Everyone gets the same items each day, starting at midnight UTC. Your first try of the day is the one that counts and the one you can share. Later tries are practice.

Something is wrong

Please open an issue on the project page and say what happened and on which device. Do not post anything private there.

Privacy

The game collects nothing. Read the privacy page.
