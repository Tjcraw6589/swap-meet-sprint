Privacy

Swap Meet Sprint, made by Bal Tak. Updated 12 November 2026.

The short version

Swap Meet Sprint collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign in, no tracking and no analytics. Nothing you do in the game is sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

The game saves your best score, your day streak, your lifetime profit, the treasures on your Finds shelf, whether sound is on and today's Daily score. This is kept on your own device (in the app's storage on iPhone, or in your browser's storage on the web) so your progress is there next time. It never leaves your device. Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's site data, deletes it.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the game makes a picture of your score and opens your device's own share sheet. You choose where it goes. The game does not see or keep what you do there.

Game portals

If you play Swap Meet Sprint on a game portal such as CrazyGames or itch.io, that website runs its own services around the game and has its own privacy policy. On CrazyGames, your progress may be saved to your CrazyGames account through their own save service.

Adverts

This version shows no adverts. If optional reward adverts are added later, this page will say which advert service is used and what it collects, before that version is released.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.
